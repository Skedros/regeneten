What Is REGENETEN

A scaffold that tells
the body to heal itself

A scaffold made from highly purified type I collagen fibers derived from bovine Achilles tendon. About the size of a postage stamp. Placed on the bursal surface of the rotator cuff, it is resorbed and replaced by new tendon-like tissue within 6 months. REGENETEN is not a patch and it is not a graft.

"REGENETEN is restorative, not reparative. If you believe in biology, this is a big step."
Felix H. "Buddy" Savoie III, MD • Chairman of Orthopaedic Surgery, Tulane University
Not a patch or graft
A bioinductive scaffold. The body generates its own new tendon-like tissue.
Delivery
Arthroscopic, suture-free. Tissue and bone anchors. Mean 13.9 minutes.
High porosity design
Swells on hydration allowing blood vessel integration and fibroblast ingrowth.
Track record
150,000+ patients globally. 10+ years of clinical use in rotator cuff repair.

How It Works

From implant to new tissue in 6 months

1
Placed on the bursal surface
Attached arthroscopically over the rotator cuff using tissue and bone anchors. Suture-free. Mean 13.9 minutes.
2
Scaffold signals biology
High porosity design swells, allowing blood vessel integration, fibroblast ingrowth, and new collagen formation.
3
Implant is resorbed
Over approximately 6 months the scaffold is completely resorbed and replaced by new tendon-like tissue.
4
New tissue remains
Approximately 2mm of new tendon-like tissue. Integrity confirmed durable at 5 years in 73% of patients.
Two applications: Used alone as an isolated treatment for partial-thickness tears, or applied over a standard repair for full-thickness tears. Restorative, not reparative.
